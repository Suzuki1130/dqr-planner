DQR Toolkit

About & credits

A fan-made community toolkit for planning and optimizing your Dungeon Quest Reborn progression.

What this is

DQR Toolkit helps you plan dungeon runs — how many clears you need for your next level, every dungeon's base EXP, farming rates, and pot upgrades. It's a fan project, not affiliated with Roblox or the Dungeon Quest Reborn developers.

Your calculator settings are stored only in your browser's local storage, on your device. Nothing is uploaded or shared.

Data sources

EXP values before Orbital Outpost come from the old Dungeon Quest wiki. Orbital Outpost through Enchanted Forest were checked in-game running Solo + HC + VIP and dividing the multiplier back out. Everything now lives in a spreadsheet the site reads from directly — see the Dungeons and Farming tabs for the links.

Dungeons from Gilded Skies onward aren't confirmed yet and follow older reference values until updated in-game.

Fan project disclaimer

Reborn is a separate community project, so treat these numbers as a close starting point rather than an official source. An official DQR Wiki is currently being put together with the team — until it's live, this toolkit and the linked spreadsheets are the best reference.
