EXP boosts

Boosts add up, they don't multiply. VIP + Solo + HC + EXP Boost is 1 + 0.2 + 0.1 + 0.1 + 1 = 2.4x, not something you'd get by multiplying them together. The two events can’t run at once.

Fill this in and you get a rough grind time.
